Why apparel

Apparel artwork has its own rules. We chose to master them.

Fabric, ink and print method all change how a design looks. A graphic that works on screen can fail on a shirt. FizEdge focuses on that gap: making artwork that holds up in production.

The same knowledge shapes our own brands. We build Coolprez as people who know what it takes to get a design onto a garment.

Rule 01

Fabric

Cotton, polyester and blends take ink differently. Fine lines and gradients have to be built for the weave, not the screen.

Rule 02

Ink

Ink spreads, layers and shifts on dark garments. We set colours, underbases and spot channels so the print matches the plan.

Rule 03

Print method

DTF, DTG, screen print and sublimation each need their own file setup. We prepare artwork for the method you print with.
