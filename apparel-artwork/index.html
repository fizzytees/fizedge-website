FAQ

Questions apparel businesses ask.

See all FAQs →
What files can you work from?

Anything you have: JPG, PNG or PDF files, screenshots, even a photo of a printed shirt. If it’s blurry or low-resolution, that’s exactly what recreation is for.

Which print methods do you prepare files for?

DTF, DTG, sublimation and screen print. If you’re not sure which one your printer uses, tell us who they are and we’ll check.

What file formats do I get?

Whatever your printer needs, usually AI, PDF, EPS, SVG or a transparent PNG.

Can you build our website or online store?

Yes, if you’re an apparel business: a print shop, clothing brand or custom apparel store. Our developer builds the site, sets up your products, payments and shipping, and helps once it’s live. Tell us what you need.

How much does it cost?

Every project is different, so we review your files first and confirm the scope and price before any work starts.

How do I get started?

Send us your artwork and a short description through the contact form, or by email at 244project@fizedge.com.
