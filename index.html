The service

Artwork that prints the way it looks.

One service covering everything between your idea and the press: new graphics, rebuilt artwork and production files set up for how you print.

  • Custom apparel graphics

    Original T-shirt graphics, typography and merch artwork, designed for fabric rather than screens.

  • Recreation & vectorisation

    Low-resolution JPG, PNG or PDF artwork rebuilt as clean, scalable vectors.

  • Cleanup & enhancement

    Jagged edges, noise, faded colour and unreadable text fixed before they reach the garment.

  • Print-ready file prep

    Sizing, transparency, colour and exports set up for DTF, DTG, sublimation or screen print.
